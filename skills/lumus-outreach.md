# SKILL: lumus-outreach

**Purpose:** Write and send personalised outreach messages for Lumus leads.

## Before you write anything

1. Read the business's website (if they have one)
2. Check their Instagram (if they have one) — note something specific
3. Read their Google reviews — look for patterns in what customers say
4. Identify the ONE biggest problem to lead with

## Tone of voice for all Lumus outreach

- Friendly but professional
- Direct and specific — never vague
- No jargon — write like you're talking to a busy business owner
- Helpful first, salesy never
- British/Irish English — "colour", "organise", "tailored", not "color", "optimize", "customized"
- Short sentences. Short paragraphs. Plenty of white space.
- Sound like a real person from Galway, not a marketing template

## What not to do

- Never start with "I hope this email finds you well"
- Never say "I wanted to reach out"
- Never use phrases like "synergise", "leverage", "holistic approach"
- Never send a wall of text
- Never ask for too much in one message (don't ask for a call AND a reply AND to visit your website)
- Never send the same message to two businesses in the same area without tweaking it

## The one-ask rule

Every message should have exactly ONE ask:
- Email A → "Would a 15-minute call be worth it?"
- Email B → "Can I send you a free snapshot?"
- DM → "Happy to share a few ideas if you're interested?"
- Follow-Up 1 → "Shall I leave you to it, or are you curious?"
- Follow-Up 2 → No ask — just close the loop warmly

## Sending cadence

| Day  | Action                    |
|------|---------------------------|
| 1    | Send Email A              |
| 3    | Send Instagram DM         |
| 7    | Send Follow-Up 1          |
| 12   | Send Follow-Up 2          |
| 14+  | No more contact — log it  |

## Tracking

After every send, update the CSV:
- Status → correct status (Email Sent, DM Sent, etc.)
- Notes → date sent + any relevant context
- Response → what they said, if anything
- Result → Pending / Positive / Negative / No Reply

## Best time to send

- Emails: Tuesday–Thursday, 9am–11am or 2pm–4pm
- Instagram DMs: Any day, 6pm–8pm (when owners check their phones)
- Avoid: Monday mornings, Friday afternoons, Bank Holidays
