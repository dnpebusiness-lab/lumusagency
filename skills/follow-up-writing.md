# SKILL: follow-up-writing

**Purpose:** Write effective follow-up messages that feel human, not automated.

## The golden rule of follow-ups

Every follow-up must add something new — a new angle, a useful piece of information, or a graceful exit.
Never just say "following up on my previous email."

## Follow-Up 1 — The Bump (Day 7)

**Goal:** Gently resurface without pressure.
**Add something new:** offer the free audit, mention something specific you noticed.

Template structure:
1. One-line acknowledgement that you sent something last week
2. New value or new angle (the free audit offer works well here)
3. Easy opt-out — "just say the word and I'll leave you to it"

## Follow-Up 2 — The Goodbye (Day 12–14)

**Goal:** Close the loop warmly, leave a good impression.
**No ask — just a warm close.**

Template structure:
1. Signal this is the last message
2. Reiterate who Lumus is in one line
3. Leave the door open for the future
4. Wish them well genuinely

## What never to write in a follow-up

- "Just circling back..." — sounds automated
- "I wanted to make sure you saw my email..." — sounds desperate
- "As per my last email..." — sounds passive-aggressive
- Any mention of "I know you're busy but..." — sounds apologetic

## When to write a third follow-up

Almost never. The exception: if they replied once (even briefly) and went quiet.
In that case, one final message referencing their specific reply is appropriate.

## Re-engagement after 90 days

Businesses that went cold get re-added to the next quarter's lead list.
Write a fresh first email — do not reference the previous contact.
Something may have changed: new owner, new season, new frustrations.

## Seasonal hooks for re-engagement

| Season      | Hook                                                          |
|-------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| January     | "New year — great time to sort the digital side of things"   |
| Spring      | "Tourist season coming — are you easy to find online?"       |
| Summer      | "Your busiest time — are you capturing all that attention?"  |
| Autumn      | "Pre-Christmas push — is your online presence ready?"        |
| December    | "New year plans — what would you like to fix in January?"    |

## Response handling

| Response type           | Action                                                    |
|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| "Yes, interested"       | Reply within 2 hours. Book a call. Move Status to Replied |
| "Not now, maybe later"  | Note the date. Re-engage in 60–90 days.                  |
| "No thanks"             | Reply: "No problem — best of luck with everything."      |
| "How much?"             | Offer a free audit first. See offer-matching.md          |
| "We already have someone"| See objection_responses.md                             |
| No reply                | Follow cadence. After Follow-Up 2, stop.                 |
