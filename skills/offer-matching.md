# SKILL: offer-matching

**Purpose:** Match each lead's specific weakness to the most relevant Lumus service and strongest commercial argument.

## Service → Weakness Matching Table

| Weakness                        | Recommended Service                      | Commercial Argument                                                 |
|---------------------------------|------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| No website                      | Website Redesign                         | Every day without a site, you're invisible to anyone searching online |
| Outdated/slow website           | Website Redesign                         | A slow or dated site actively damages trust before they even contact you |
| No Google Business Profile      | Google Business Profile Optimisation     | 80% of local searches happen on Google — if you're not there, you don't exist |
| Low Google reviews (<20)        | Reputation Management (Reviews)          | Customers read reviews before choosing — you're losing them to competitors with more |
| No Instagram                    | Instagram Content & Management           | Visual businesses need a visual presence — your competitors are winning on Instagram |
| Inactive Instagram              | Social Media Audit & Strategy            | Inconsistent posting destroys credibility — looks like the business isn't active |
| Poor photography on website/GBP | Photography & Visual Content             | Bad photos cost sales — professional visuals are the fastest trust-builder |
| No email list or newsletter     | Email Marketing Setup                    | You have no way to reach past customers — email is the cheapest retention tool |
| Weak brand identity             | Brand Identity & Visual Refresh          | Inconsistent branding reads as unprofessional — first impressions happen online now |
| Hard to find on Google          | Local SEO                                | If you're not on page 1 for local searches, you're not getting those customers |

## Priority rule

If a business has multiple weaknesses, pick the ONE that:
1. Is most visible to their customers (biggest impact on trust/bookings)
2. Lumus can fix fastest (shows quick value)
3. Has the clearest ROI story

**Default priority order:**
1. No website → Website Redesign
2. No Google Business Profile → GBP Optimisation
3. No Instagram → Instagram Content & Management
4. Multiple weaknesses → Full Digital Presence Audit

## How to frame the offer

Never say: "We offer website design services."

Say: "Most [sector]s in [location] with no website are losing 30–40% of enquiries to competitors who are easier to find. We can have you online in 3 weeks."

Always connect the service to a specific business outcome: more bookings, more footfall, more enquiries, more trust.

## Pricing guidance (for when prospects ask)

Do not quote prices in cold outreach.
When asked, offer a free audit first, then provide a tailored quote.
Mention "we work with local businesses so we price accordingly" — this signals you're affordable without committing to a number.
