# SKILL: local-lead-research

**Purpose:** Find local business leads for Lumus in Galway, Mayo and surrounding areas.

## What this skill does

Given a location and sector, find real businesses that match the Lumus target profile:
- Operating in Galway, Liosban, Salthill, Oranmore, Westport, or Mayo
- In a target sector (café, restaurant, pub, B&B, hotel, barber, hair salon, beauty salon, gym, clinic, takeaway, independent retail)
- Likely to have weak digital presence

## Research sources to check

1. **Google Maps** — search "[sector] in [location]", look at results with few reviews or low-quality photos
2. **Instagram** — search hashtags like #galwaycafe, #salthillrestaurant, #westportpub
3. **TripAdvisor** — restaurants and hospitality, look for low-review-count listings
4. **goldenpages.ie** — Irish business directory, filter by location and category
5. **Booking.com** — for B&Bs and hotels in Mayo and Galway
6. **Facebook** — local business pages, check engagement levels
7. **Yelp.ie** — beauty, health, restaurants

## Signals of a weak digital presence (good lead)

- Fewer than 30 Google reviews
- Google rating below 4.0
- No website listed on Google Maps
- Website URL leads to a broken or very old-looking site
- No Instagram account (or last post was over 3 months ago)
- No Facebook page, or page has under 200 likes
- Photos on Google Maps are low quality or outdated
- No menu/services listed online

## Output format

For each business found, record:
- Business Name
- Sector
- Location
- Website (or "none")
- Instagram handle (or "none")
- Google Business Profile URL (or "none")
- Email (if visible on their site or Google listing)
- Phone
- Main Weakness (what specifically is weak — be specific)
- Notes (anything useful for personalising the outreach)

## Research targets per week

Aim for 25–40 businesses per week across all locations.
Prioritise HOT leads (multiple digital weaknesses) over borderline cases.

## Exclusions

- Do not add chain businesses (Supermac's, McDonald's, Costa etc.)
- Do not add businesses that clearly already have excellent digital presence
- Do not add businesses outside the target locations
