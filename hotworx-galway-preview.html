The honest bit

A gym membership you never use isn't a fitness plan. It's a direct debit.

Most people don't quit because they're lazy. They quit because the workout never fits the day — too far, too long, too rigid. So we made it fifteen to thirty minutes, three people to a sauna, and unlocked the door round the clock. Turn up when your life lets you.

What actually happens

Three things,
start to finish.

01

Book a class in the app

Nine isometric classes run 30 minutes, three HIIT classes run 15. Scroll the timetable, tap the slot that suits, done. No queue, no front-desk shuffle.

02

Step into the sauna

You and up to two others. A screen on the wall runs the session start to finish, so there's no guessing what comes next. The infrared heat does the part you can't see.

03

Leave, and check your numbers

The app logs the calories you burned and keeps your streak honest. Come back tomorrow morning, or tonight — the studio doesn't close.
