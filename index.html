Questions

Questions you probably
have before getting in touch.

How much do projects usually cost?

It depends on what you actually need — a small site is very different to a full brand and website. After a short discovery call we send a clear, fixed price for your specific scope, so there are no surprises. If something is outside your budget, we'll say so honestly.

How long does a website take?

Most websites take a few weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on size and how quickly we get content and feedback from you. We agree milestones up front so you always know the next date in the calendar.

Can you help if I already have branding?

Yes — plenty of clients come to us with an existing logo and colours they like. We'll work with what's already in place and just sharpen the parts that aren't pulling their weight. If something genuinely needs replacing, we'll tell you why.

Do you work with small businesses?

Absolutely. A lot of our work is with restaurants, coffee shops, hospitality and local service businesses. Size isn't the filter — we just want to be sure we can genuinely help before we take a project on.

What happens after launch?

Once your site is live, there's a short review window included so we can fine-tune anything based on how it's performing in the real world. After that, we're here whenever you need updates — no contract or retainer required.

How do we start?

Send us a quick message using the form below or book a free discovery call. We'll reply within one business day with a few honest questions, and if it's a fit we'll send a clear proposal soon after. No pressure, no hard sell.
