# Lumus — Objection Handling Scripts

Use these when prospects reply with common objections.
Keep responses short, human, and pressure-free.

---

## "We already have someone doing our website / social media"

```
That's great — sounds like you're already thinking about it.

Out of curiosity, are you happy with the results you're getting from it?
If it's working well, brilliant. If there's any part that feels like it's not
performing as well as you'd like, that's exactly the kind of thing we help with.

Either way, happy to do a quick no-obligation check and give you an honest view.
Worth 10 minutes?
```

---

## "We're too busy right now"

```
Completely understand — busy usually means things are going well, which is great.

When things do calm down, the online presence piece tends to be the thing
that keeps the busy periods coming. Happy to drop you a note in a month or two
if that would be more useful?
```

---

## "We don't have the budget for this"

```
Fair enough — budget is always a real consideration.

A few things worth knowing:
- We work with small businesses, so our pricing is built around that
- We can usually start with one thing (e.g. Google Business Profile)
  rather than overhauling everything at once
- Happy to give you a free audit first so you know exactly what the return
  would look like before spending anything

Would that be useful?
```

---

## "We tried digital marketing before and it didn't work"

```
I hear that a lot, and honestly it's usually because the approach wasn't
right for the type of business — not because it can't work.

Local businesses like yours need local-focused strategy, not generic
social media posting or ads. What we do is quite different.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call where I show you specifically
what we'd do differently? No commitment.
```

---

## "Just send me some information"

```
Of course — I'll keep it short and useful.

[Attach or paste the relevant service one-pager]

The most relevant thing for [Business Name] would be [RECOMMENDED SERVICE].
Happy to answer any questions or put together a more specific plan if anything
jumps out at you.
```

---

## "How much does it cost?"

```
Depends on exactly what's needed — we don't do one-size-fits-all pricing.

To give you an honest number, I'd want to take a look at where things are
and what would actually make a difference. That's a free 20-minute exercise
on our end.

Want me to run that and come back to you with a figure?
```

---

## No reply at all (after Follow-Up 2)

Do not send a third follow-up. Mark as "Follow-Up 2 Sent" in the CSV.
Set Result to "No Reply". Add a note: date last contacted.
These go into a "re-engage in 90 days" list for Step 1 next quarter.
