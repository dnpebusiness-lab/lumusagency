## TIPS FOR PERSONALISATION

1. **Always use the business name** — never "Hi there" without it if you have it
2. **Reference something specific** — a photo you saw, a dish on their menu, something real
3. **Keep it short** — under 150 words for email, under 80 for DM
4. **One ask per message** — don't ask for a call AND a reply AND a review
5. **Sound human** — read it aloud before sending; if it sounds like a template, rewrite it
