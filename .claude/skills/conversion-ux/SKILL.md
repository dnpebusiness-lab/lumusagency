# conversion-ux

## Purpose
Design landing pages that convert. Every section must have a job. Every element must earn its place.

## Core Principles

### Above the Fold
- The primary value proposition must be clear within 3 seconds
- Hero must include: headline, subheadline, primary CTA, secondary CTA
- No slider carousels in the hero
- No full-screen video backgrounds that delay loading
- Hero visual must reinforce the message, not distract from it

### CTA Strategy
- Primary CTA repeated at least 3 times: hero, mid-page, final section
- Secondary CTA in hero only (not repeated)
- CTA text must be action-specific: "Book an AI Audit", not "Learn More"
- Primary CTA colour must be the accent colour
- CTA button must be visible without scrolling on desktop

### Trust Signals
- Do not use fake testimonials, fake reviews, fake statistics or fake awards
- Use process transparency instead (show how it works clearly)
- Use specific language instead of vague claims
- Use FAQ section to remove objections
- Plain language makes the page feel more trustworthy than marketing language

### Page Flow
- Problem before solution. Show the pain first.
- Solution must follow directly from the problem identified.
- Process section removes uncertainty ("how does this work?")
- Benefits section is specific, not vague.
- Services section is clear and scannable.
- FAQ section handles the last remaining objections.
- Final CTA closes with clarity and confidence.

### Friction Reduction
- Do not ask for too much information in forms
- For booking CTAs: keep to name and email as minimum
- Do not use popups that appear immediately on load
- Do not use cookie banners that block content

### Mobile Conversion
- CTA button must be easy to tap (minimum 44px height)
- Font size minimum 16px for body on mobile (prevents zoom)
- No horizontal overflow
- Key message visible without scrolling more than one screen on mobile

### Scannability
- Use short paragraphs (2 to 3 sentences max)
- Use bullet points for lists of 3 or more items
- Section headers must explain the section purpose clearly
- Cards must be readable at a glance

## Application
Apply this skill to the page structure, CTA placement, copy length, form design and overall conversion logic.
