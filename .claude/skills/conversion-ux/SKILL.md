# Skill: Conversion UX

## When to Use
Run this skill when a page has traffic but low enquiry or sale rates, when the path from arrival to action is unclear, when forms feel long or risky, or when trust signals are absent or buried. Also run before any page launch where the primary goal is a contact, booking, or purchase.

## Goal
Remove every piece of friction between a visitor and the desired action. Make the next step obvious, the risk feel low, and the reward feel clear at every point on the page.

## Strict Rules
- Every page must have exactly one primary CTA. If there are multiple, one must be visually dominant
- The primary CTA must appear above the fold on both desktop and mobile without scrolling
- CTA button labels must describe the action and the outcome ("Book your free call" not "Submit")
- No form should ask for information the business does not need before first contact (no company size, no budget range, no "how did you hear about us" on a cold enquiry form)
- Trust signals (testimonials, credentials, number of clients, guarantees) must appear within scroll distance of the CTA, not only at the bottom of the page
- No auto-playing video or audio
- No pop-up that fires in the first 5 seconds
- Mobile tap targets must be at least 44×44px
- No CTA hidden behind a carousel or tab the user must interact with to see

## What to Check
- **Primary CTA:** visible above fold, specific label, high contrast button
- **Value proposition:** is it clear within 5 seconds what the visitor gets and why here, not elsewhere?
- **Trust signals:** testimonial with name and result, credential, guarantee, or social proof — close to the CTA
- **Form friction:** number of fields (aim for 3 or fewer on first contact), field labels visible (not placeholder-only), clear submit label, privacy note
- **Mobile conversion path:** test on 375px — CTA visible without scroll, tap targets large enough, no layout overlap on form
- **Objection handling:** does the page address the main reason a visitor would leave without acting?
- **Page speed perception:** does above-the-fold content load before 2 seconds? (flag large unoptimised hero images)
- **Exit friction:** is there anything that pulls attention away from the conversion goal (excessive nav links, unrelated CTAs, social media icons in the header)?

## Output Format
For each issue found, output:

**Location:** [element or section]
**Issue:** [what is broken or missing]
**Fix:** [specific change to make]
**Impact:** [High / Medium / Low] — based on how directly it blocks conversion

End with a short prioritised action list: the three fixes most likely to move conversion rate, in order.
