# Skill: Anti-AI Copywriter
## Project context
Single-file landing page: `index.html`. Lumus is a one-person creative studio in Galway, Ireland (est. 2024). It sells brand identity, website design, social content and graphic design to premium local businesses — restaurants, hospitality, coffee shops, local service businesses. Founder works alone. No team. Direct contact only.

## When to use
Run this skill when any copy on the page sounds like it could belong to any agency in any country. Specific triggers already present in this file:
- "we light the way" (tagline — says nothing)
- "Look more premium and memorable" (adjective-only, no fact)
- "Stay visible and consistent" (filler phrase, zero specificity)
- "Turn visitors into enquiries" appears twice verbatim
- "Built around trust, not agency theatre" (self-congratulatory)
- Any sentence starting with "We believe", "We are passionate", "We help businesses"

## Goal
Every sentence must be true, specific, and written for one real type of business in Galway and the west of Ireland. The tone is confident without being boastful, direct without being blunt, warm without being casual. It sounds like a skilled person talking — not a brand voice document.

## Strict rules
- Banned words: passionate, innovative, cutting-edge, seamless, robust, leverage, empower, solutions, holistic, bespoke, elevate, unleash, transform, game-changing, world-class, next-level, journey, ecosystem, synergy, curated, tailored, premium (when self-applied as an adjective about Lumus itself)
- No sentence that starts with "We believe" or "We are committed to"
- No abstract claim without a specific fact behind it ("fast turnaround" → how many weeks; "experienced" → what kind of projects; "affordable" → a price or audit offer)
- CTAs must say what happens next ("Book a free 30-minute discovery call" not "Get started")
- The word "premium" is allowed only when describing the client's business — never to describe Lumus itself
- Read every adjective. If a fact could replace it, use the fact
- The tagline "we light the way" must be evaluated — flag it for replacement unless there is a specific reason it stays
- The portfolio note "Every project below is a concept website" must be handled — either remove it or reframe so it does not undermine credibility at the worst possible moment

## What to check (section by section)
- **Tagline / eyebrow:** does it say something specific about what Lumus does or who for?
- **Hero H1:** does it name the outcome for a specific type of client?
- **Hero sub:** does it add one concrete detail (location, specialisation, number, process)?
- **Service descriptions:** each one currently ends with a vague promise — rewrite to describe the deliverable ("A complete identity: wordmark, colour palette, type system, and a one-page brand guide")
- **Metrics (24h / direct / selective):** these are claims, not proof — flag any that need a specific fact to back them up
- **FAQ answers:** these are the best copy on the page — protect them, do not water them down
- **Portfolio descriptions:** currently 5–8 words each — too thin to build trust, too thin to explain the work

## Output format
**Location:** [section name or element]
**Before:** [exact current text]
**After:** [rewritten text]
**Why:** [one sentence]

List all changes top to bottom. End with a one-paragraph summary of the overall tone shift.
