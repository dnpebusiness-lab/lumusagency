# Skill: Anti-AI Copywriter

## When to Use
Run this skill when website copy sounds generic, vague, or artificially enthusiastic. Triggers: phrases like "unleash your potential", "cutting-edge solutions", "we're passionate about", "take your business to the next level", "seamless experience", "robust", "leverage", "empower", or any sentence that could belong to any company in any industry.

## Goal
Replace AI-flavored filler with copy that is specific, human, credible, and written for one real audience. Every sentence must earn its place by saying something true and useful.

## Strict Rules
- Never use: passionate, innovative, cutting-edge, seamless, robust, leverage, empower, solutions, holistic, bespoke, elevate, unleash, transform, game-changing, world-class, next-level, journey, ecosystem, synergy, curated, tailored
- No exclamation marks in body copy
- No sentence that starts with "We believe" or "We are committed to"
- No abstract claims without a specific fact behind them ("fast" → how fast; "experienced" → how many years or projects; "affordable" → a price or range)
- Headlines must say what the page does for the reader, not what the company thinks of itself
- CTAs must be action-specific ("Book a free 30-minute call" not "Get started")
- Read every sentence aloud. If it sounds like a brochure, rewrite it

## What to Check
- Hero headline: does it name the audience and the result they get?
- Subheadline: does it add a specific detail the headline did not cover?
- About/intro section: does it include at least one concrete fact (year founded, number of clients, location, specialisation)?
- Service descriptions: does each one describe the actual process or deliverable, not just the outcome?
- Testimonials: are they specific (name, company, result) or generic praise?
- CTAs: are they specific about what happens next?
- Entire page: find every adjective and ask whether a fact could replace it

## Output Format
For each piece of rewritten copy, output:

**Location:** [section name or element, e.g. "Hero headline"]
**Before:** [exact original text]
**After:** [rewritten text]
**Why:** [one sentence explaining what was wrong and what the fix does]

List all changes in page order, top to bottom. After the last item, add a one-paragraph summary of the overall tone shift made.
