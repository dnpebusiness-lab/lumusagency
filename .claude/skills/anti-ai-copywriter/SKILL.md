# anti-ai-copywriter

## Purpose
Write copy that sounds human, direct and credible. Not AI-generated. Not corporate. Not full of buzzwords.

## Core Rules

### Tone
- Direct and confident. Short sentences.
- Human voice. Write like a smart person talking to another smart person.
- Strategic. Not cheerful. Not enthusiastic. Not salesy.
- Serious without being cold.

### Banned Words and Phrases
Never use:
- unlock the power of
- revolutionise your business
- seamless solutions
- elevate your workflow
- future proof your business
- empowering businesses
- cutting edge technology
- innovative solutions
- tailored to your needs
- next generation AI
- game changer
- transformative
- leverage
- holistic approach
- end to end solutions
- bespoke
- synergies
- at the forefront
- driving growth
- take your business to the next level
- harness the power
- scalable solutions
- digital transformation
- robust
- streamline
- world class

### What to Write Instead
Use language around:
- save time
- remove repetitive work
- build cleaner systems
- respond faster
- automate follow ups
- reduce manual mistakes
- turn scattered tasks into workflows
- make AI useful inside the business
- build practical systems, not hype
- hours saved per week
- less manual work
- fewer dropped leads
- cleaner process
- faster response
- less admin

### Structure Rules
- Hero headline: one clear, specific idea. Under 12 words ideally.
- Subheadline: expands the headline. One or two sentences. Concrete benefit.
- Body copy: short paragraphs. Two to three sentences max per block.
- CTA copy: direct action verb. Not "Get Started". Use "Book an AI Audit", "See What Can Be Automated", "Start the Audit".
- FAQ answers: one to three sentences. Simple. Honest. Not promotional.

### Avoid
- Metaphors that sound clever but say nothing.
- Questions used as headlines ("Are you tired of...?")
- Three-word power phrases as section titles (unless clearly warranted)
- Overpromising results or guarantees
- Fake urgency ("Act now, limited spots!")
- Exclamation marks.

## Application
Apply this skill to every headline, subheadline, body paragraph, CTA, FAQ answer and card copy written in the project.
