# website-art-director

## Purpose
Direct the visual design of premium websites. Ensure every design decision is intentional, editorial and high value.

## Visual Direction

### Colour System
- Use a dark base (#07070F or similar deep dark)
- One primary accent (electric blue #4A7CF8 or similar)
- One secondary accent (violet #8B5CF6 or gold #F59E0B)
- Text: white (#FFFFFF) and muted white (rgba(255,255,255,0.55))
- Borders: rgba(255,255,255,0.07) to 0.12
- Do not use blue/purple gradient backgrounds for hero sections
- Avoid neon cyan, hot pink, lime green accents

### Typography
- Use editorial display fonts for headlines: Syne, Clash Display, Space Grotesk, or similar
- Use clean sans-serif for body: Inter, DM Sans, or similar
- Headline size: 56px to 80px desktop, 36px to 52px mobile
- Line height for headlines: 1.05 to 1.15
- Letter spacing for headlines: -0.02em to -0.04em
- Body size: 16px to 18px
- Strong typographic hierarchy: headline, subheadline, label, body, caption

### Layout
- Use 12-column grid or clear section rhythm
- Large hero sections with generous vertical padding (120px to 180px)
- Section padding: 80px to 120px vertical
- Strong negative space between elements
- Cards with consistent padding (24px to 32px)
- No overcrowding. Every element needs space to breathe.

### Components
- Glass panels: dark frosted glass using backdrop-filter: blur(). Use sparingly.
- Cards: dark surface (#0E0E1A or similar), subtle border, 12px to 16px border radius
- Buttons: primary with solid accent fill, secondary with border only. No rounded pill unless intentional.
- Section labels: small caps or uppercase tracking, muted colour, 11px to 13px

### Avoid
- Stock photo backgrounds
- Generic hero backgrounds (city skylines, abstract waves)
- Cartoon icons or 3D mascots
- Overuse of glassmorphism
- Blue to purple gradient blobs as hero background
- Multiple conflicting accent colours
- Drop shadows that look cheap (use glow or subtle elevation instead)
- Inconsistent border radii

### Elevation System
- Level 0: flat surface (background)
- Level 1: cards (#0E0E1A)
- Level 2: modals or overlays (#141428)
- Level 3: tooltips or floating elements
- Use box-shadow with the accent colour for glow effects (low opacity)

## Application
Apply this skill to every design decision in the project. Every colour, font, spacing value, card style and visual element must be checked against these rules.
