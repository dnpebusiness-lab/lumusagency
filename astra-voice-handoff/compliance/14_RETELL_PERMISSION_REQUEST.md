# Retell commercial permission request

Send to Retell Sales/Partner team before onboarding a paying restaurant.

Subject: Written confirmation for multi-tenant restaurant SaaS use of Retell AI

Hello Retell team,

We are building Astra Voice, an Ireland-based multi-tenant SaaS for restaurants. Each restaurant configures its own AI receptionist, approved knowledge, phone number and call dashboard. Retell would be used as the replaceable voice-orchestration provider behind our own application, while Twilio/SIP and our database remain separately controlled.

Before proceeding commercially, we need written clarification because section 10.1 of the current Terms of Service restricts resale of AI-generated voice outputs and use of Retell as a substitute or intermediary layer.

Please confirm whether Retell authorises the following model:

1. Astra charges restaurants a monthly subscription that includes access to an Astra-branded dashboard and a Retell-powered voice agent.
2. Astra manages multiple restaurant/location agents through Retell APIs; restaurant customers do not receive Astra's Retell credentials.
3. Calls are inbound customer-service and reservation calls, not outbound marketing.
4. Each restaurant is the controller for caller data and Astra acts as its processor.
5. Astra requires a DPA including SCCs, documented subprocessors, retention/PII controls and deletion support for EU callers.
6. Astra may display Retell-derived transcripts, summaries and call outcomes inside its customer dashboard.
7. Astra may commercially describe the service without using Retell trademarks unless separately approved.

Please provide:

- the appropriate partner/reseller/OEM agreement or written waiver;
- commercial pricing for multi-tenant use;
- data-processing locations and available regional routing;
- confirmation of no model training on our caller content when the relevant opt-out/privacy controls are enabled;
- retention and deletion options for audio, transcripts, logs and backups;
- current DPA/SCCs and subprocessor list;
- incident-notification commitment and security documentation;
- any restrictions we must reflect in our customer agreement.

We will not launch the Retell-powered service to paying restaurants until this is confirmed in writing.
