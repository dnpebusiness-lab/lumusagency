## What happens when you call

At the start of the call, you will be told that you are speaking with an AI assistant. The assistant converts speech into text so it can understand and respond, answer approved restaurant questions, manage a requested booking and decide when a member of staff is needed.

For the pilot, Astra does **not intentionally store an audio recording**. Voice data is still transmitted through the telephone and voice providers to conduct the live conversation. The restaurant may keep:

- your phone number and call time;
- a transcript of the conversation;
- a short summary, language, reason for calling and outcome;
- your name, contact details and booking information where you request a reservation;
- security and audit records.

Do not provide payment-card numbers or unrelated sensitive information during the call.

## Allergies and health information

If you mention an allergy or health requirement, the transcript may contain sensitive health information. The AI will not decide whether food is medically safe for you. Serious allergy, cross-contamination or uncertain requests are escalated to restaurant staff. The restaurant must state here the legal basis and Article 9 condition approved by its solicitor/DPO: `[complete before launch]`.

The restaurant uses the data to answer your request, take steps towards or manage a reservation, provide customer service, investigate failures and protect the service. The legal bases relied on must be completed by the restaurant: `[Article 6 basis/bases and legitimate-interest summary]`.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability. Contact `[restaurant privacy email]`. The restaurant may need information to verify that the call relates to you. You may complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission: https://www.dataprotection.ie/

## Automated decision-making

The service is not intended to make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects. It may answer, suggest availability, prepare a booking or escalate to staff. A booking is confirmed only after the relevant booking system reports success.

## Changes

Material changes to purposes, recording, providers or retention require an updated notice and, where appropriate, a renewed DPIA.
