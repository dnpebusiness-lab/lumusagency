## Service

Astra provides a configurable AI telephone receptionist and dashboard. The precise enabled functions, locations, phone numbers, booking provider and retention settings are listed in the order form.

The service is assistive and may make errors. It is not an emergency service, medical adviser, legal adviser or substitute for the restaurant's statutory food-information duties.

## Restaurant operational duties

The restaurant must:

- keep opening hours, prices, policies and allergen declarations complete and current;
- verify and explicitly approve each item before it is available to the voice agent;
- provide legally required written allergen information through its own menu/signage/website;
- provide a trained human escalation route during agreed periods;
- review call outcomes and promptly report unsafe or false responses;
- configure and approve caller notices and lawful bases;
- use the service only for lawful inbound restaurant interactions in V1.

## Safety and allergens

The agent may communicate only approved factual allergen declarations linked to a specific item. It must not infer safety, guarantee “allergen-free”, give medical advice or decide whether a dish is safe for an individual. Severe allergy, uncertainty or cross-contamination questions must be transferred or escalated.

The agreement must allocate responsibility for source-data accuracy, software controls, incident handling and customer communications. A solicitor must draft liability and indemnity wording; do not copy generic caps without insurance review.

FSAI source: https://www.fsai.ie/business-advice/running-a-food-business/allergens/allergen-information-for-non-prepacked-food

## AI and recording transparency

The restaurant authorises a greeting that identifies the assistant as AI from the start. Audio recording is off by default. Any later recording activation requires written approval, a legally reviewed announcement/consent flow and corresponding database configuration.

## Data protection

The Article 28 DPA, approved subprocessors, retention schedule and privacy notices form part of the agreement. Each party will fulfil the role allocated by actual processing activities.

## Availability and support

Define:

- target availability and excluded maintenance;
- severity levels and response targets;
- planned maintenance notice;
- vendor outage/fallback behaviour;
- service-credit mechanism if offered;
- no guarantee that every accent, noisy environment or request will be understood.

## Intellectual property and vendor rights

- Restaurant retains its menu, brand and content.
- Astra retains software, templates and platform IP.
- Rights in vendor-generated outputs depend on vendor terms.
- No Retell-powered customer service may launch until Retell confirms the commercial SaaS/reseller use in writing.

## Fees and fair use

State subscription, included minutes, telecom/vendor pass-through costs, tax/VAT treatment, overages, failed-payment handling and price-change notice.

## Suspension and termination

Permit immediate suspension for unsafe allergen data, security incidents, unlawful use, non-payment or vendor/legal prohibition. Define export window, deletion timetable, phone-number portability and assistance at exit.

## Warranties, liability, indemnities and insurance

Solicitor to complete after confirming:

- professional indemnity and cyber coverage;
- Retell/Twilio contractual caps and exclusions;
- food/allergen risk allocation;
- indirect loss and data-protection treatment;
- limits that remain enforceable under Irish law;
- pilot status and no-production representations.

Proposed: Ireland; courts of Ireland. Solicitor to confirm.
