Your property,
managed properly.

We handle everything a short-term rental requires — from the moment a guest enquires to the moment they leave — so you can earn from your property without running it yourself.

Enquire about your property

The challenge

Short-term letting is work.
It doesn't have to be yours.

Guest enquiries come in at all hours. Check-ins need coordinating. Properties need cleaning to a consistent standard. Maintenance issues need local contacts. Listing platforms need updating. Most property owners who manage this themselves spend more time on it than they expected.

City Hosting takes the operational side off your plate. We handle guest communication, coordinate cleaning, manage check-in and check-out, and deal with any property issues that come up — so you're not on call.

How it works

From first conversation
to managed property.

  1. 1

    Initial conversation

    Get in touch and tell us about your property — where it is, how it's currently used, and what you're hoping to achieve. This takes 15 minutes and there's no obligation at this stage.

  2. 2

    Property visit and assessment

    We visit the property to assess its condition, suitability, and rental potential. We'll give you an honest view on expected income, what the property needs to be guest-ready, and what management would look like day-to-day.

  3. 3

    Agreement and setup

    If we agree it's a good fit, we sign a management agreement, prepare the property, create the listing, and handle photography. You're not committed to anything until this stage.

  4. 4

    Live and ongoing

    Bookings open, guests start arriving, and we manage everything. You receive a monthly income report and can see your calendar. We contact you only when something genuinely needs your decision.
