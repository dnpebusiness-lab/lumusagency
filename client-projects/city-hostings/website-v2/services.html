What we offer

How we work
with property owners.

Most owners want the whole thing handled: guests, cleaning, pricing, reporting. Some already manage their own listing and just need someone local for arrivals. If you're not sure yet, we'll visit and give you an honest view before you commit to anything.

What's included

Everything, or just
what you need.

Guest communication

All enquiries, booking confirmations, pre-arrival messages, in-stay support and check out handled directly, plus guest review management. No automated replies.

Property care

Professional cleaning between every stay, linen and towel management, pre-arrival checks, and consumables restocking. Maintenance issues spotted and coordinated before they become problems.

Listing, pricing and reporting

Listing creation and ongoing optimisation, pricing strategy based on seasonality and demand, calendar management, and a monthly income report so you always know where things stand.

Guest services only

Already manage your own listing and pricing? We can handle just the guest-facing side: arrivals, key handover, in-stay contact, and check out. Listing and pricing decisions stay with you. Can be combined with cleaning coordination on request.

Free property assessment

Not sure short term letting is right for your property? We visit, assess condition and local demand, and give you a realistic income range and current Fáilte Ireland registration requirements. No obligation to proceed.

Owner control, always

Full access to your property calendar and personal-use blocking, an annual performance review, and a direct point of contact. If something isn't working for either of us, we'll say so.
