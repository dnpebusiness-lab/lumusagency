What we offer

Services for property owners
in Galway.

Three service tiers for different ownership situations. All built around honest assessment, local knowledge, and consistent guest care.

01

Full management

Everything. Start to finish.

For owners who want to rent their property without being involved in the daily. We handle every aspect of the rental operation, from listing to guest checkout and everything in between.

Guest management

  • All guest enquiries answered
  • Booking confirmation and pre arrival communication
  • Arrival coordination and key handover
  • In stay support for guest queries
  • Check out process
  • Guest review management

Property operations

  • Professional cleaning between stays
  • Linen and towel management
  • Pre arrival property check
  • Maintenance issue identification and coordination
  • Consumables (toiletries, essentials) restocking
  • Seasonal property review

Listing and income

  • Listing creation and ongoing optimisation
  • Pricing strategy based on seasonality and demand
  • Calendar management
  • Monthly income reporting to owner
  • Owner calendar access and personal-use blocking
  • Annual performance review
Enquire about full management
02

Guest services only

For owners who manage
their own listing.

Some owners manage their own listings and pricing but need someone physically local to handle guest-facing operations. This tier covers arrivals, guest contact, and property access. Nothing more, nothing less.

What's included

  • Guest arrival coordination
  • Key handover and access management
  • In stay point of contact for guests
  • Check out handling
  • Immediate issue escalation to owner

What's not included

  • Listing management
  • Pricing decisions
  • Cleaning coordination (unless separately arranged)
  • Maintenance sourcing
  • Guest review management

Guest services can be combined with cleaning coordination on request.

Discuss guest services
03

Property assessment

Before any commitment,
an honest view.

Not sure if short term letting makes sense for your property? We visit, assess, and give you an honest picture of income potential, what the property needs, and whether management is the right fit. No obligation to proceed.

What we assess

  • Property condition and guest-readiness
  • Location and local demand
  • Realistic income range for the property type
  • What the property needs before going live
  • Regulatory considerations (planning, Fáilte Ireland)
  • Whether full management is the right option

What you receive

  • A plain-language summary of our findings
  • Estimated income range based on property and location
  • A clear view of what setup would involve
  • Recommendation on next steps, with no pressure either way

The assessment is free and carries no obligation to proceed with management.
