What we do

Full-Service Airbnb Management
So You Can Earn Without the Hassle.

From pricing to cleaning to guest communication, we handle every part of running your property, while you enjoy the income.

Dynamic Pricing, Maximum Returns

With over 5.6 million listings on Airbnb alone, standing out means staying sharp on price. We adjust your rates daily based on demand, local events, and market shifts, so you're never leaving money on the table.

More Bookings, Less Vacancy

We list your property across Booking.com, Expedia, Priceline and Airbnb, not just one platform, giving you maximum exposure and higher occupancy year-round.

Hotel-Standard Cleaning, Every Turnover

Our in-house housekeeping team ensures every guest arrives to a spotless property, with fresh linen and toiletries between every stay.

Clear, Monthly Reporting

No guesswork. Each month you get a detailed breakdown of occupancy, pricing, and revenue, so you always know exactly what your property is earning.

Free Photography & Property Evaluation

Before you go live, we visit your property to take professional photos and give you tailored recommendations to help it stand out to guests.

Hassle-Free Maintenance

Minor maintenance under €150 is handled automatically, no back-and-forth needed. Anything above that, we'll contact you first for approval.

No Hidden Fees

We charge a flat 20% of net revenue (after VAT), plus a small one-off onboarding fee after your first month. No ongoing charges, no surprises.
