How it works

From first conversation
to managed property.

  1. 1

    Initial conversation

    Tell us about your property: where it is, how it's currently used, what you're hoping to achieve.

  2. 2

    Property assessment

    We visit the property, assess its condition and location, and give you an honest view on rental potential and what management would involve.

  3. 3

    Agreement and setup

    If it's a good fit, we agree terms, get the property ready, and create the listing. No obligation before this point.

  4. 4

    Ongoing management

    We handle everything from the first booking. You receive regular updates and monthly income reports.
