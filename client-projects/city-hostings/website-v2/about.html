Who we are

A Galway company
that knows Galway.

City Hosting is a property management company based in Galway. We manage holiday lets and short-term rentals for property owners who want their properties well looked after — and their guests well treated.

Why City Hosting exists

Short-term letting in Galway has grown significantly. More property owners are choosing to rent their properties to visitors rather than long-term tenants — but managing that process properly takes time, local knowledge, and consistent attention.

City Hosting was set up to fill that gap. We manage the operational side — guest communication, cleaning coordination, check-in, maintenance — so property owners can earn from their properties without being on call.

How we work

We work with a small number of properties at a time. That's deliberate. We'd rather manage fewer properties properly than take on more than we can look after well.

Every property we manage gets the same level of attention — regular checks, consistent cleaning standards, and a point of contact for guests who need something. We don't use automated guest messaging, and we don't outsource the care of your property to people we haven't vetted.

Where we work

We manage properties in Galway City, County Galway, and the wider West of Ireland — areas we know well. That local knowledge matters. We understand the seasonal patterns, the neighbourhoods, what guests are looking for when they visit Galway, and who to call when something needs fixing fast.

We consider additional western locations on a case-by-case basis, depending on property type and our capacity to manage it properly.

What we won't do

We won't take on a property we don't think is suitable for short-term letting, or one we can't manage to our standard. We won't make promises about income we can't back up. And we won't disappear after the management agreement is signed.

If something isn't working — for you or for us — we'll say so directly.

Our approach

Three things we take seriously.

  1. 1

    Honest assessment

    Before taking on any property, we visit, assess, and give the owner a genuine view of what's possible — including the things that might make short-term letting the wrong choice for their situation.

  2. 2

    Consistent property care

    A property managed by us is checked before and after every stay. Cleaning standards are maintained — not just for the first booking but for every booking. Maintenance issues are caught early and dealt with properly.

  3. 3

    Straightforward owner communication

    Monthly income reports. Prompt contact when something needs your decision. Access to your property calendar. No surprises, no noise. You stay informed without being on call.
