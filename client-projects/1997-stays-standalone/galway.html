Questions

We send a PIN code to your email before arrival. You enter it at the door — no front desk, no waiting. Access is available 24 hours a day. If you have any issues, there's a number to call.
There is street parking on Newcastle Road and the surrounding streets. Paid car parks are available close to the city centre. We'd recommend checking current parking arrangements on arrival as availability varies.
Galway University Hospital is approximately a 2-minute walk from the front door. This makes 1997 Stays one of the most convenient places to stay for anyone visiting patients or working at the hospital.
Show your booking confirmation at the Mocha Beans counter. The 10% discount applies to everything on the menu — coffee, food, pastries. No voucher code needed. Works for the duration of your stay.
Check-in is from 3pm. Check-out is by 11am. Because check-in is contactless, you're not tied to someone being at the desk — the PIN works as soon as your check-in time arrives.
Yes. Fast broadband is available throughout the property. The WiFi details are in the welcome information you'll receive before arrival. It works reliably for remote work, video calls and general use.
Three of the four rooms have private en-suite bathrooms. The Triple Room has a private bathroom directly across the hallway — it's not shared with other guests. Details are listed on each room page.
