Our story

Born from a refusal
to settle for average.

Volta started as one obsession: find the world’s best green coffee and extract it perfectly. Every single time. No chains. No blends designed for consistency over quality. No day-old beans dressed up with flavoured syrups.

We seat 22. It stays that way on purpose. When the queue gets long, we’d rather you wait than rush a shot.

What sets us apart

Three things we never
cut corners on.

Other coffee bars talk about quality. We’ve built the entire operation around three non-negotiables that most places don’t even think about.

01

Origin first.

Every bean is traceable to the farm, the harvest, the elevation. We rotate origins monthly so you taste the world, not just a generic blend calibrated for inoffensiveness.

02

Dialled in daily.

Every morning before we open, we dial in each group head. Temperature, pressure, extraction time. It changes with the humidity. Most bars don’t bother. We can’t imagine not.

03

Space that serves.

22 seats. No background music after 10am. Good light. A counter you can watch the work from. Some things matter more than square footage and Instagram-friendly neon.
