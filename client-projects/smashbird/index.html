      Our story

      Make the
      mess count.

      Smashbird is a family-run street-food brand from Galway. Built around serious flavour, an unusually deep sauce range and no interest in playing it safe.

      Family
      run

      Independent. Local. Not a franchise.

      Galway
      based

      Cross Street and Liosbán. Two spots, one identity.

      Sauce
      first

      The sauce range is the competitive difference. Not an afterthought.

      Smashed
      patties

      Pressed hard on a flat-top. Lacy edges. No exceptions.

      Fried
      chicken

      Real crunch. Worth the mess.

      Vegan
      & GF

      Options that belong in the same conversation, not in a footnote.

      Smashbird does not do subtle. The food is built to be immediate, textural and worth talking about. The sauce range is deeper than it needs to be. The menu keeps changing. The attitude does not.
