# Phase 2 — Brand & Positioning

## Objective
Transform the client brief into a complete brand strategy that defines positioning, tone of voice, messaging framework, and brand pillars. This document controls everything that follows.

## Responsible Agent
Brand Strategy Agent (`/agents/02-brand-strategy-agent.md`)

## Required Input
- Approved Client Brief (v1+)
- Any existing brand materials
- Competitor website URLs

## Process

1. Orchestrator briefs Brand Strategy Agent with approved client brief
2. Brand Strategy Agent analyses brief and develops strategy
3. Brand Strategy Agent produces Brand Strategy Document (v1)
4. Orchestrator reviews for specificity, differentiation, and usability
5. If approved → version and save. If not → specific revision instructions.

## Expected Output

File: `[client-slug]-brand-strategy-v1.md`
Saved to: `client-projects/[client-slug]/02-brand/`

Contents:
- Positioning statement
- Genuine differentiator
- Audience insight
- Brand pillars (3–4)
- Tone of voice with examples
- Key messages (5–6)
- What the brand is NOT
- Competitive positioning
- Messaging summary for next agent

## Quality Gate

Do NOT approve and move to Phase 3 unless:
- [ ] Positioning statement is specific to this one business only
- [ ] Genuine differentiator is something competitors cannot claim
- [ ] Audience insight reveals something real, not obvious
- [ ] Brand pillars have practical expression in website content
- [ ] Tone of voice examples are concrete (show, don't tell)
- [ ] Key messages reflect what the audience cares about
- [ ] Zero generic phrases anywhere in the document
- [ ] "What the brand is NOT" is specific enough to use as a filter

## Common Issues to Watch For

- Tone of voice that sounds like every other brand in the category
- Differentiator that is actually just a category descriptor ("great food", "beautiful rooms")
- Key messages that are what the client wants to say vs what the audience wants to hear
- Brand pillars that are too abstract to guide practical decisions
