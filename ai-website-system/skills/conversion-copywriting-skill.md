## SKILL INSTRUCTIONS

### CONVERSION COPYWRITING PRINCIPLES

#### The 4 Jobs of Copy on a Local Business Website

1. **Attract** — Confirm to the right visitor that this site is for them
2. **Engage** — Make them want to know more (create desire)
3. **Convince** — Overcome doubt and build trust
4. **Convert** — Give them an obvious, compelling reason to act now

Every section of copy does at least one of these four jobs. If a piece of copy doesn't clearly do one, cut it or rewrite it.

---

### THE HEADLINE HIERARCHY

Great headlines do ONE of these things:

1. **State a specific benefit** — "Rooms with views of Bantry Bay. Breakfast included."
2. **Name the situation** — "Planning a weekend in Connemara?"
3. **Make a specific promise** — "Every dish made from scratch, every day."
4. **Ask a qualifying question** — "Looking for somewhere different for Sunday lunch?"
5. **Make a bold, specific claim** — "The only farm-to-table B&B in the area."

Bad headlines do ONE of these things:
- Try to be clever without communicating anything
- State the obvious ("Welcome to [Business Name]")
- Make a vague claim ("Excellence in every detail")
- Talk about the business instead of the customer

---

### THE CTA FORMULA

**Format:** Action verb + specific outcome (+ optional friction reducer)

| Generic CTA (bad) | Conversion CTA (good) |
|-------------------|-----------------------|
| Book Now | Reserve Your Table |
| Contact Us | Get a Free Quote |
| Learn More | See the Full Menu |
| Click Here | Check Room Availability |
| Submit | Send My Enquiry |
| Get In Touch | Call Us Now — We're Open |

**The friction reducer:** A short line below or beside the CTA that removes the last objection.
- "No booking fees. Cancel anytime."
- "Response within 24 hours."
- "Free consultation, no obligation."
- "We don't charge until the work is done."

---

### WRITING FOR THE AUDIENCE (NOT THE BUSINESS)

The biggest mistake in small business copy: the business talks about itself.

**Business-focused (bad):**
> "We have been serving the Killarney area for 23 years, offering a wide range of services to our valued customers."

**Audience-focused (good):**
> "Twenty-three years of fixing what others said couldn't be fixed. If you've been told no elsewhere, call us first."

**Rule:** Every paragraph should contain more "you" and "your" than "we" and "our".

---

### HANDLING OBJECTIONS IN COPY

Every potential customer has objections. Good copy addresses them before they have to ask.

Common objections for local businesses:
- "Is this too expensive for me?" → Address with price transparency or value framing
- "Will I get a table / room?" → Address with booking ease and availability communication
- "Is this legit / trustworthy?" → Address with social proof, credentials, real photos
- "Is this worth the trip?" → Address with the unique experience promise
- "What if I don't like it?" → Address with guarantees, flexible cancellation, reviews

---

### THE SPECIFICITY RULE

Specificity is the most powerful conversion tool in copy.

Vague claims don't convert. Specific details do.

| Vague (bad) | Specific (good) |
|-------------|----------------|
| "Years of experience" | "Established in 1997" |
| "Award-winning" | "Named Best Café in Connacht, Good Food Ireland 2023" |
| "Local ingredients" | "Our beef comes from Pat Flannery's farm, 3km down the road" |
| "Great views" | "Views of the Twelve Bens from every bedroom window" |
| "Many options available" | "14 rooms, from cosy singles to our Harbour Suite" |
| "Quick response" | "We reply within 2 hours, Mon–Sat" |

### THE ANTI-AI TEST

Before submitting any copy, run the Anti-AI Test:

Read each paragraph and ask: "Could an AI have written this about any generic business?"

If YES → It needs to be rewritten with:
- A specific detail only this business would have
- A reference to a real person, place, or experience
- Language that matches the brand's specific tone
- An insight about the audience that shows you understand them

---

### COPY RHYTHM AND VOICE

For premium local business websites:

- **Sentence length:** Vary it. Short sentences hit hard. Longer sentences give context and texture. Use both.
- **Paragraphs:** Never more than 3–4 sentences for body copy. White space is your friend.
- **Active voice:** Always. "We cook every dish to order" not "Every dish is cooked to order."
- **Second person:** "You'll find" not "Guests will find"
- **Contractions:** Generally yes — makes copy feel more human. "We're" not "We are". Match the brand tone.
- **Jargon:** None. Write like you're explaining to a friend.

---

### COPY CHECKLIST (RUN BEFORE SUBMITTING)

- [ ] H1 on each page communicates something specific (not just the business name)
- [ ] Zero filler phrases ("passionate about", "dedicated to excellence", "in today's world")
- [ ] All CTAs use action verb + specific outcome
- [ ] Every paragraph has more "you" than "we"
- [ ] At least 3 specific details in each page that could only belong to this business
- [ ] Objections are addressed without being asked
- [ ] Copy read aloud: sounds like a real, confident person
- [ ] Passes the Anti-AI Test
