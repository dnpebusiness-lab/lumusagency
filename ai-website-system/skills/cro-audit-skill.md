# Skill: CRO Audit

## What This Skill Does
Loads a structured conversion rate optimisation audit framework into the CRO Agent. Ensures the review is systematic, evidence-based, and produces specific actionable improvements.

## When to Use
Activate for Phase 9 (CRO Review) on every project. Also use for quarterly CRO reviews during maintenance.

## Agent That Uses It
CRO Agent (Phase 9) + Maintenance Agent (quarterly reviews)

---

## SKILL INSTRUCTIONS

### THE CRO AUDIT FRAMEWORK

#### Step 1: Define the Conversion Goal

Before reviewing anything, state clearly:
- Primary conversion: [e.g., "visitor submits contact form"]
- Secondary conversions: [e.g., "visitor clicks phone number", "visitor clicks booking link"]
- Micro-conversions: [e.g., "visitor views menu", "visitor scrolls to contact section"]

Everything in the audit is evaluated against whether it helps or hurts these goals.

---

#### Step 2: The 5-Question Framework

For every page, answer these 5 questions:

**Q1: CLARITY** — Does the visitor know what this is, who it's for, and what to do next within 3 seconds?
- Test: Cover the business name. Can you still tell what this business does?
- Test: Is there a clear next action? Is it obvious what to click?

**Q2: TRUST** — Is there enough evidence to make the visitor feel confident?
- Check: Social proof (reviews, testimonials, ratings)
- Check: Credibility signals (years in business, certifications, real photos)
- Check: Risk reducers (cancellation policy, no booking fees, guarantee)

**Q3: FRICTION** — What makes it harder than necessary to convert?
- Check: Number of form fields
- Check: Number of clicks to complete the primary action
- Check: Any confusing navigation or dead ends
- Check: Mobile usability of the conversion path

**Q4: MOTIVATION** — Is there a compelling reason to act NOW rather than later?
- Check: Is the value proposition specific and compelling?
- Check: Any legitimate urgency (seasonal availability, limited capacity)?
- Check: Are benefits communicated, not just features?

**Q5: DISTRACTION** — Is anything pulling attention away from the primary conversion?
- Check: Multiple competing CTAs
- Check: Outbound links leading users away
- Check: Visual noise or complexity
- Check: Navigation items that lead to dead-end pages

---

#### Step 3: Element-by-Element Audit

**Above the fold (every page):**
- [ ] Business value proposition visible immediately
- [ ] Primary CTA visible without scrolling
- [ ] No confusion about what to do next

**Headlines:**
- [ ] Every H1 communicates a specific benefit
- [ ] No headline that could appear on a competitor's site
- [ ] No clever wordplay that sacrifices clarity

**CTAs:**
- [ ] Primary CTA uses specific action language
- [ ] Primary CTA is visually distinct
- [ ] Only one primary CTA per section (not competing)
- [ ] CTA appears multiple times on long pages
- [ ] CTA copy tells them what they get, not just what to do

**Trust signals:**
- [ ] Real reviews with real names and context
- [ ] Photo of the owner or team (face of the business)
- [ ] Specific credentials (not vague "award-winning")
- [ ] No stock photography where real photos should be

**Forms:**
- [ ] Minimum fields required (5 or fewer for simple enquiry forms)
- [ ] Every field is labelled (not just placeholder)
- [ ] Submit button copy is specific ("Send Enquiry" not "Submit")
- [ ] Confirmation message after submission
- [ ] Form works on mobile (no zoom-in required)

**Mobile conversion path:**
- [ ] Phone number click-to-call and above fold
- [ ] CTA meets 44px minimum tap target
- [ ] Contact accessible within 2 taps from any page
- [ ] No accidental taps from crowded elements

---

#### Step 4: Write the Priority Matrix

For each issue found, categorise:

| Priority | Definition |
|----------|------------|
| High | Directly blocking conversions. Fix before launch. |
| Medium | Reducing conversion rate. Fix within 2 weeks. |
| Low | Marginal improvement. Test and measure. |

For each recommendation:
- Quote the specific element
- Explain exactly what is wrong
- Provide the exact fix with copy examples where relevant
- Estimate the impact

---

#### Step 5: Specific Fix Format

Always write recommendations in this format:

```
ISSUE: [Name]
PAGE: [Exact page]
SECTION: [Hero / CTA section / Form / etc.]
CURRENT STATE: [Quote or describe exactly what's there now]
PROBLEM: [Why this hurts conversion — be specific]
FIX: [Exact action]
EXAMPLE:
  CURRENT: "Book Now"
  IMPROVED: "Reserve Your Table — It's Free to Cancel"
PRIORITY: [High / Medium / Low]
```

---

#### Common CRO Wins for Local Businesses

1. **Phone number visible on mobile above fold** (often the #1 conversion action)
2. **Specific CTA copy** ("Reserve a Table" vs "Book Now")
3. **Real testimonial with name and context** replacing generic reviews
4. **Contact form with 3 fields** instead of 8
5. **Price or price range visible** (removes the biggest objection)
6. **Friction reducer below CTA** ("No booking fees", "Reply in 24 hours")
7. **Owner/team photo** (face of the business builds instant trust)
8. **Reviews count or star rating** visible near primary CTA
9. **Opening hours on every page** (not just the contact page)
10. **Map visible without scrolling** on contact page
